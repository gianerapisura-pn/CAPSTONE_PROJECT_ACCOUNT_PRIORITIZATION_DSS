# Visual and Explanation Inventory - 2026-10-30

## Shared Interaction Contract

`frontend/components/result-explanation.tsx` remains the shared explanation system. Explained values support pointer hover, keyboard focus/Enter/Space, touch, and Escape. Their portal sits outside table clipping, stays open while the pointer/focus is inside, and follows scrolling. Saved-value details are expandable. Chart tooltips use the chart's actual saved point/series values; nearby controls or links provide keyboard/touch alternatives to chart selection.

Account-specific explanations receive the saved row and its reference date where appropriate. Dates and categorical labels are dimensions/source context; no fictitious numerical formula is assigned to a year, account name, or verification status. Generic count definitions are accompanied by the table/row population and section context. Unavailable values are not displayed as numerical zero.
